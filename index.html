Our Story

Honest food, cooked with care

Savory began as a twelve-seat counter with one simple idea: cook the best of each season, simply and generously. Today our kitchen works with more than sixty local farmers, fishers and producers to bring the freshest ingredients to your table.

Chef Elena Marquez and her team blend classic technique with bold, modern flavours. Expect wood-fired meats, handmade pasta and vibrant plant-forward dishes, alongside a bar programme built around small-batch spirits.

  • Wood-fired kitchen

    Open flame cooking for deep, smoky flavour.

  • Farm to table

    A menu that changes with the seasons.

  • Craft cocktails

    Seasonal drinks from our in-house bar team.
